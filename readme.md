# Metro Bank Customer Complaint Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes Metro Bank’s customer complaints data using Microsoft Excel to generate actionable insights for customer experience improvement and fraud risk mitigation.

The objective was to move beyond simply counting complaints and instead identify operational inefficiencies, service gaps, and high-risk fraud cases.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
  - Pivot Tables
  - Calculated Columns
  - Conditional Formatting
  - Charts
  - Trend Analysis

## Business Problems Solved

### Customer Experience Analysis
- Identified the most frequent complaint types
- Analyzed complaint volume trends over time
- Calculated average complaint resolution time
- Determined which complaint categories take the longest to resolve
- Measured complaint backlog (Open and In Progress cases)
- Analyzed aging of unresolved complaints

### Customer & Product Correlation
- Examined complaints by customer segment (Retail vs Corporate)
- Analyzed complaints by region
- Linked complaint types to specific banking products
- Assessed whether high-balance customers are experiencing more issues

### Risk & Fraud Analysis
- Calculated total number of fraud cases reported
- Identified active fraud cases (Open and In Progress)
- Flagged high-risk complaints (Open fraud cases older than 7 days)

## Key Insights
- Certain complaint categories indicate process inefficiencies due to longer resolution times
- Backlog aging highlights service bottlenecks
- Fraud-related complaints require stronger prioritization controls
- High-value customers may represent potential retention risk if issues persist

## Project Outcome
This analysis provides structured insights that support:
- Improved customer service efficiency
- Reduced fraud exposure
- Better prioritization of high-risk complaints
- Stronger data-driven decision-making
